Layla uses an emerging technology that allows ChatGPT-like chatbots to run locally on your phone without an internet connection, so it requires a considerable amount of resources.

The expected response time for Layla Full on a high-end phone such as an **S23 Ultra with 12 GB of RAM** is around one or two words per second. If you have a high-end phone and are experiencing slow response times, try one or all of these methods:

- Close all background apps.
- Turn off battery optimisation for Layla. Navigate to *Settings* → *Apps & notifications* → *Layla* → *Advanced* → *Battery* → *Battery optimization*, then choose *Don't optimize*.
- Turn on high-performance mode or gaming mode in your phone's settings, if available.
- Switch to the Q2 base model. It has roughly the same intelligence as Layla Full, with a performance boost.

**RAM is only one part of the equation: once you have enough, adding more does not help. The rest is up to your CPU.**

Here is a rough estimate of the response speeds you can expect:

- **12 nm CPU chipset:** The Tiny model generates one or two words per second; other models are generally impractical.
- **6 nm CPU chipset:** The Lite model generates one or two words per second; the Full model may generate only a few words per minute.
- **4 nm CPU chipset:** The Full model generates one or two words per second. The Lite model is almost as fast as cloud AI providers.

For more information on supported phones, visit [Layla Help and Support](https://www.layla-network.ai/contact).
