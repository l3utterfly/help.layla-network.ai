Use these checks when Layla does not behave as expected.

## A model does not load

Confirm that the model download completed and that your device has enough available memory. Restarting Layla can clear a failed loading attempt.

## Responses are slow

Large models require more processing and memory. Try a smaller quantization or reduce the active context length.

## The issue continues

Record the Layla version, device model, Android or iOS version, and the name of the affected AI model before requesting support.
