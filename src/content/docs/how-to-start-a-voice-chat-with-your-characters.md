Layla supports combining speech-to-text and text-to-speech to conduct a voice chat with your characters. This feature is particularly exciting when used with animated characters. It truly feels like you're having a video chat!

## How Voice Chat Works in Layla

Voice Chat first listens for your voice, then uses speech-to-text technology to convert it into text. This text is fed into the LLM, which generates a response based on your input. Once the LLM has crafted a reply, text-to-speech technology brings that response to life by reading it aloud. This entire process runs automatically in the background while your character's animated background remains displayed in the foreground.

## How to Enable Voice Chat in Layla

To use this feature, enable the *Voice Chat* app in Layla. If you need guidance, read [How to enable or disable mini-apps within Layla](/how-to-enable-disable-mini-apps-within-layla/).

### Steps to Start a Voice Chat

The video shows these steps:

1. Begin a voice chat by selecting *Voice Chat* in the menu at the top right of the screen.
2. The interface will clear, focusing solely on the character.
3. Allow a moment for the text-to-speech module to initialise.
4. Once the main button turns green, start speaking.
5. After you finish speaking, give the AI a short moment to process your input.
6. The character's voice will respond shortly after. You will notice that the character's lips move in sync with the voice.

### Tips for a Better Experience

- **Speak clearly:** Ensure your voice is clear and articulate for the best recognition by the speech-to-text technology.
- **Use a quiet environment:** Background noise can interfere with voice recognition. Find a quiet place for your voice chat.
- **Allow processing time:** After your speaking turns, give the AI a moment to process before speaking again.

Following these tips can help make conversations with your characters more fluid and engaging.

### Conclusion

Voice Chat provides another way to interact with animated characters in Layla. By combining speech-to-text and text-to-speech, it simulates a video-chat experience and opens up new creative possibilities for character conversations.
