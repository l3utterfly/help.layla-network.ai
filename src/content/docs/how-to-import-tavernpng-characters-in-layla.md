Layla supports importing characters from external websites using TavernPNG. These are special images that contain information about your character.

Let's go to [chat.layla-cloud.com](https://chat.layla-cloud.com/) to download some characters! You can also use other sites that support TavernPNG, such as Chub.AI.

Open any character, scroll to the very bottom, and tap the **Download as TavernPNG** button. This will save the image to your phone.

Go back to Layla, go to **Choose Personalities**, scroll to the bottom, and tap the plus button. This brings up the character editor.

Go to the **Advanced** tab and tap the button to import a TavernPNG image.

Choose the downloaded image from your phone. This automatically fills in all the character information for you!

Now you can start a chat with them!
