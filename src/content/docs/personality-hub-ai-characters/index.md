**The Personality Hub is a built-in Layla feature for browsing and downloading AI characters that the assistant can role-play as during your chats—running fully offline, with no account or personal information required.**

> **Browse and download characters:** [Open the Personality Hub at chat.layla-cloud.com →](https://chat.layla-cloud.com/)

[![Gallery of characters available through Layla's Personality Hub.](./personality-hub-gallery.png)](https://chat.layla-cloud.com/)

The characters come from a range of backgrounds, including TV shows, books, and films, and you can add as many as you like to your own list.

This article explains what the Personality Hub is, how it compares with cloud-based character apps, how to open it and download characters, and how to share your own creations.

## What is the Personality Hub?

The Personality Hub is a shared collection of characters, or personalities, that Layla can take on. Each one changes how the AI talks, what it knows about itself, and how it behaves in conversation. Downloading a character adds it to your personal list, so you can select it whenever you want a particular kind of chat.

Because Layla runs on-device, the characters you download chat with you offline. Once a personality is on your phone, it does not need an internet connection to talk.

## A private, offline alternative to Character.AI

Most character chat happens through cloud services such as [Character.AI](https://character.ai) or [Chub](https://chub.ai), which require an account and run your conversations on someone else's servers. The Personality Hub takes a different approach. Both downloading and uploading are anonymous: there is no sign-up, email, or personal information to hand over.

For anyone looking for a Character.AI alternative that keeps conversations on their own device, this is the main difference. Your chats with a downloaded character stay local, the catalogue is shared by the community rather than locked to one provider, and there is no subscription to use it.

## How to open the Personality Hub

The Personality Hub is in Layla's Apps section. To open it:

1. On the Character Selection screen, tap the **Apps** icon in the top-right corner.
2. Choose the **Personality Hub** app.
3. Browse the catalogue and open any character that interests you.
4. Tap **Download** to add the character to Layla.

After a character downloads, it appears in your characters list, ready to chat whenever you want.

You can also access the web version at [chat.layla-cloud.com](https://chat.layla-cloud.com).

## Sharing your own characters

If you have built a character of your own, you can publish it to the Personality Hub for other people to download. The upload process is anonymous in the same way as the download process: no account and no personal details. Your character joins the shared catalogue so the rest of the community can find it, while nothing identifying is attached to it.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Personality Hub in Layla?

It is a built-in catalogue of AI characters you can download into Layla. Each character gives the assistant a distinct personality to chat with, and all of them run offline once downloaded.

### Is the Personality Hub a Character.AI alternative?

Yes. It serves a similar purpose—browsing and chatting with a range of characters—but it runs on-device, requires no account, and does not send your conversations to a cloud service.

### Do I need an account to download characters?

No. Both downloading and uploading are anonymous. There is no sign-up and no personal information is required.

### Do downloaded characters work offline?

Yes. Layla runs on-device, so once a character is downloaded, it can chat with you without an internet connection.

### Where do downloaded characters appear?

They appear in your characters list. Once a download finishes, the character shows up there and you can start a chat with it like any other personality.

### Can I share a character I made?

Yes. You can upload your own creations to the Personality Hub anonymously, and other users can then download them.

## Putting it together

The Personality Hub gives Layla a steadily growing library of characters to take on, shared by the community and downloaded to your phone with a single tap. Because everything runs on-device, the characters you collect stay with you offline on Android, without an account or subscription.
