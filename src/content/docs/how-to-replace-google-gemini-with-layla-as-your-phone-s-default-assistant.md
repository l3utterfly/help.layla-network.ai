As of version v6.1.0, Layla can be your phone's default assistant!

Here is Samsung's tutorial on [how to change the default assistant on your Samsung phone](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANS10001575/).

![Samsung Settings screen used to reach the default assistant setting.](/assets/articles/how-to-replace-google-gemini-with-layla-as-your-phone-s-default-assistant/samsung-default-assistant-settings.jpg)

Voilà! When you long-press the power button, Layla will come up as your default assistant!

You can set a default character in Layla, so the assistant that gets brought up is your own character. This gives a new dimension to your personal assistant on your phone.
