Layla v6 supports local *Agents* that run on your device, giving Layla the ability to use tools and communicate with external services.

This article gives an overview of how Agents work in Layla and what you can do with them!

**The Agent Mini-app**

Agentic functionality can be unlocked in Layla by installing the **Agents** mini-app, found in the Browse Apps screen.

![Layla Agents mini-app overview.](./agents-mini-app-overview.jpg)

Download and install the **Agents** mini-app in Layla. When you open the app, you will see a wide variety of pre-created Agents that you can use immediately.

![List of pre-created Agents in the Agents mini-app.](./agents-list.jpg)

*These tools are already created and working for you. You don't need to edit them.* In fact, you can go back and start a new chat with Layla, and they will work automatically!

![Layla using the DuckDuckGo News and Web Reader Agents in a chat.](./duckduckgo-agent-chat.jpg)

In the screen above, you can see the *Web Reader* and *DuckDuckGo News Results* Agents activated, bringing you the latest news from the internet!

Layla's Agents can do many more things, like scheduling an event in your calendar, creating alarms and reminders, sending emails, sending SMS messages, and much more. Feel free to explore the Agents mini-app for a list of all the available Agents.

**Enabling and disabling agents**

By default, any Agents enabled in the *Agents mini-app* are available for Layla—the character—to use. If you don't want Layla to access an Agent, you can disable it by turning off the switch beneath its icon.

You can also attach Agents to your custom characters. To do this, go to the *Advanced* tab in the character creator:

![Advanced tab in the Create Character screen, showing the Select Agents control.](./character-advanced-agents.jpg)

Tap the *Select Agents* button, and a list of all available Agents will appear. Select the ones you wish to attach to your character. Only the Agents you select will be enabled.

Sometimes, you don't want your characters to access all Agents. Too many Agents may confuse your character, or you may want a character specifically for roleplay and don't want to break immersion by having them "search the web" in the middle of a session. Layla gives you full customisability to use Agents as you wish.

*A quick note: we refer to Layla as both the app as a whole and "Layla" as the default character, identified by the butterfly logo. This is the character used if you don't create your own characters.*

**Creating your own agents**

Layla gives you the ability to create your own tools and Agents. These Agents can be customised to fit your needs or integrate with your own services.
