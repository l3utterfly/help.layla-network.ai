Here's how to download characters from Layla's Personalities Hub!

First, go to the **Personalities Hub** mini-app. You can scroll through thousands of characters here!

Select one that you'd like to chat with. Open it and tap the download button at the top-left or at the bottom.

Save your new character by tapping the button at the top-right.

Go back to the main screen and tap **Choose Personalities**.

Scroll down to find your new character and start a conversation!
