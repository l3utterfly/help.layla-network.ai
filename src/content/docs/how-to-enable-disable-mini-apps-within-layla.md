Layla comes with many features that are not enabled by default. These features are optional, but they are always available for you to use if you need them.

<video controls playsinline src="/assets/articles/how-to-enable-disable-mini-apps-within-layla/browse-mini-apps.mp4"></video>

These features are organised in Layla as **mini-apps**. Mini-apps are like the regular apps you use on your phone, but they are contained within Layla and designed to work with Layla's features.

Mini-apps can provide features ranging from configuring characters to enabling image generation and much more.

You can find a list of your currently installed apps by tapping the *My Apps* button on any of the screens shown below:

![My Apps button in Layla.](/assets/articles/how-to-enable-disable-mini-apps-within-layla/my-apps-button.jpg)

You will see a list of your currently enabled apps:

![My Apps screen listing enabled Layla mini-apps.](/assets/articles/how-to-enable-disable-mini-apps-within-layla/my-apps-screen.jpg)

The plus or minus sign in the top-right corner allows you to browse all the features Layla has to offer.

*Please note: all mini-apps require the premium version of Layla. You can purchase the premium version through Google Play or from the purchase screen in the direct APK version. Layla offers a one-time purchase to unlock all features; no monthly subscription is required.*
